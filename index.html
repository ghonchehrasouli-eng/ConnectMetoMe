0:00—
Emotions are neurobiological responses of our body to our thoughts, inner talks or outer circumstances. In either way, they are real. All emotions are valuable whether they are desirable or undesirable. The undesirable ones work as an alarm or indicator that there is an unmet basic need that wants our attention, care and love. Mind Filter
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Our mind filter is the lens through which we interpret every experience. It is shaped by our core beliefs, inner voice and past experiences. The mind filter determines what we notice, how we interpret events, and what meaning we give to our experiences. By becoming aware of our mind filter, we can begin to question the stories our mind creates and find a more compassionate and accurate understanding of ourselves and our world.

Inner Voice
🎧 Listen
0:00—
What is inner voice and how does it impact us? Our inner voice is the ongoing internal narrative — the running commentary in our mind about ourselves, others and the world. It is shaped by our core beliefs, past experiences and the voices of significant people in our lives. The inner voice can be critical, supportive, fearful or compassionate. Becoming aware of our inner voice is the first step to transforming it into a kinder, more supportive presence.

Unworthiness / Shame
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Can you recognize what mechanisms you use to feel worthy, adequate and enough? Unworthiness and shame are uncomfortable feelings to be experienced. So, in early age, mechanisms were learnt to hide them and prevented them from coming into the surface. Why we use blaming and criticizing? To be in control, to progress and avoid mistakes. However, for some people the painful direction of blaming focus is themselves — and for some others it is focused on others. The outcome is very different, but the root cause feelings are the same. The Unworthiness and Shame Spectrum: on the self-focused side we have perfectionism, overachiever, self-sabotaging, self-blaming. On the other-focused side we have entitlement, self-absorbed, overachiever, others-sabotaging, others-blaming, others-ridiculing.

Codependency
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Can you identify your codependency spectrum — do you go into a caregiver role or a caretaker role? Codependency is a pattern where we focus on others' needs at the expense of our own, or we demand others meet our needs. The Caregiver side: makes others happy and comfortable at the expense of making themselves uncomfortable and under pressure. Caring for others as if their needs and wants are more important than their own. Loose boundaries with others to not make them disappointed. The Caretaker side: makes themselves happy and comfortable, often demanding from others. Both patterns come from the same root — unmet needs and difficulty being a responsible adult for ourselves.

Core Beliefs Practice
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Core beliefs have mainly formed in early childhood based on our experiences and our mind interpretation from our experiences. These beliefs are located in the basement of our mind and are accepted as absolute truth deep down. To find core negative beliefs: Remember an undesirable event that happened in the past recent months which makes you upset and resented. First: How did you feel? Label your feelings. Where do you locate these feelings in your body? What kind of physical sensation does it carry? Second: What was your inner talk? Third: What were your beliefs behind it? Do the same process for argument with parents and siblings, and bold undesirable life circumstances like major loss of career or relationship. Can you find the footprint of main beliefs behind all of them?
