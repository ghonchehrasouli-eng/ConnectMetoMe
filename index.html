Life is like a moderate or difficult hiking with beautiful sceneries. For hiking, we equip ourselves with proper clothing, shoes, tools and snacks to enjoy the path in spite of facing challenging unpredictable trail. Do we need to do the same in our life? Do we need to properly equip ourselves with skills, coping strategy and self development to go through the challenging path? What we do when our mind sky gets cloudy, stormy with painful undesirable feelings: lonely, anxiety, frustration, shame, uncertainty and fear? When we face challenges in our relationship with others? When our criticizing mind gives us the not good enough message by sabotaging us, or our victim mind gives us miserable feelings? To be able to drive a vehicle, we need to get a driving license. Otherwise the car crash and damaging ourselves and others would be inevitable even with a Maserati. But in life we expect to drive our life and manage our mind and relationships automatically. Connect Me To Me is a tool to help us gain self development skills and get familiar with some of our mind functionality that leads to mind refining, transformation and resilience.

Why Connect Me To Me?

Practicing being a responsible adult for ourselves generates stronger inner connection and enhances the quality of relationship with ourselves. Improving inner relationship enhances quality of our outer relationship and quality of our life.

Connect Me To Me helps us go through the path of mind refinery and take our power back by:

  • ✓Taking responsibilities as an adult for our feelings, moods, needs and wants
  • ✓Being a supportive compassionate caring Adult for ourselves and be our own source of love and security
  • ✓Recognizing and transforming our demons: inner narration back of our mind into a loving caring voice
  • ✓Recognizing and transforming our mind's filter

Inner Child
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Who is our inner child? The inner child is part of our personality that relates us to our past experience's memories, and associated feelings, to the feeling of playfulness, creativity and joy. This child isn't a separate person living inside of us, but a representation of our early childhood memories and our relationship with those memories. Also, it's a representation of our patterns and way of thinking and behaving that generated inside of us from those days. Based on our inner child perception from our early experiences, she or he might play a powerless victim or entitled prince or king who's center of the world and many other roles. Also, based on same perception, that little girl or boy might be wounded by unmet physical or emotional needs through abandonment, neglect, lack of support and security.

We become a supportive compassionate adult when we take responsibility for our feelings and inner child feelings — secured, loved, cared and supported — and when we soothe and comfort her or him exactly as supporting compassionate parents do. Emotions are neurobiological responses of our body to our thoughts, inner talks or outer circumstances. In either way, they are real. Inner negative voice is a whispering voice at the back of our mind. We identify ourselves with this voice and our thoughts and cannot differentiate them from ourselves as they sound so real. Inner negative talk gives meaning to our experience. The voice pretends what it is telling us is truth and makes us believe it. Codependency
🎧 Listen
0:00—
Can you identify your codependency spectrum — do you go into a caregiver role or a caretaker role? Codependency is a pattern where we focus on others' needs at the expense of our own, or we demand others meet our needs. The Caregiver side: makes others happy and comfortable at the expense of making themselves uncomfortable and under pressure. Caring for others as if their needs and wants are more important than their own. Loose boundaries with others to not make them disappointed. The Caretaker side: makes themselves happy and comfortable, often demanding from others. Both patterns come from the same root — unmet needs and difficulty being a responsible adult for ourselves.
